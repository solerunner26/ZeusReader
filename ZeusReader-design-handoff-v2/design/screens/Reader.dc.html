Chapter 4 · Water in Soil

How water moves upward

Soil is not a solid block but a maze of particles and the spaces between them.

Capillary action draws water upward through narrow soil pores against gravity, supplying plant roots during dry intervals.

The finer the pores, the higher the water climbs. In sandy soil the pores are wide, so water drains quickly and rises only a little.

Clay holds water close to the roots for longer, which is why gardeners mix the two.
