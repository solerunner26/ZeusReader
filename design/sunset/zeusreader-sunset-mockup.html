Chapter 4

How water moves upward

Soil is not a solid block but a maze of particles and the spaces between them.

action draws water upward through narrow soil pores against gravity, supplying plant roots during dry intervals.

The finer the pores, the higher the water climbs. In sandy soil the pores are wide, so water drains quickly.

Palette with a job

Alabaster and white by day, obsidian and charcoal by night. The accent pair is the only colour, and it marks what you can do next: active tabs, progress, the main button.

Depth, not clutter

Soft 24px shadows lift cards off the canvas, and radial glows sit behind the hero objects. Starburst halos are reserved for wins and the reading card.

Rewarding motion

Buttons spring to 96% when pressed, rings and bars fill on load, lists rise in a stagger and confetti falls only on results. Reduce motion turns it all off.

Reading stays quiet

The page itself is plain paper with Literata type. Decoration lives around it, never on top of the words.
